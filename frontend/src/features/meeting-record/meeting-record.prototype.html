Meeting Record playback

Throwaway behavior prototype. Does one shared Meeting timeline, with independent video and audio selection, behave as you expect when a citation jumps, a track is missing, or access disappears?

This demo uses invented participants and transcript lines. It has no recordings, streaming connection, AI, or access enforcement. Each button changes the proposed playback state for review. It is not production UI.

    Selection never changes anyone's position on the timeline. Empty video selection means audio-only playback; empty audio selection means silent playback. There are no download controls.

    Invented transcript and AI-note citations

    A citation seeks every track together, preserves your selections and play/pause choice, and identifies the cited speaker. A hidden or muted speaker stays hidden or muted until you change your selection.

    Guided walkthroughs

    Pick a walkthrough to reset the demo, then press its steps in order. The controls above also work freely. Media gaps contribute silence or an unavailable tile; buffering of a selected, available track stalls the shared timeline until it can resume.
